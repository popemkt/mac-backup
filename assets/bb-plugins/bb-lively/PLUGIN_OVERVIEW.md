Quick, subtle motion for bb, on whatever theme you use.

## What you get

- **Press.** Buttons sink a touch while you hold them and spring back when you let go.
- **New messages rise in.** A row that arrives at the end of a thread fades in and rises a
  few pixels. Rows you scroll back to, and a thread you switch to, never animate.
- **Menus and dialogs** open on a quick spring from where you opened them, and close faster.
- **Send pulse.** Sending ripples the send button; the Send/Stop icon pops as it swaps.
- **Thread status pulse.** A thread in the sidebar that just finished, failed, or started
  waiting for you rings twice in green, red, or yellow.
- **Composer focus glow**, **toast spring**, **switch and checkbox** motion, and a gentle
  **hover lift** on cards.
- Optional: an **accent caret** in the composer, and **calm bb's own motion** under reduced
  motion (bb has no reduced-motion setting of its own).

Everything is 90–220 ms, moves only position, scale and opacity, and stays out of the way of
bbim: while bbim's hints are up, nothing moves.

## Settings

- **Motion:** System (follows your OS "reduce motion"), Full, Reduced (short fades only, no
  movement), or Off.
- **Speed:** Snappy, Normal, Relaxed.
- One switch per effect.

The settings page also lists any of bb's own markup lively relies on that has not shown up
in this window, so a bb update that breaks an effect is visible instead of silent.

## Themes

Two optional palettes in Settings → Appearance: **Lively** (warm neutrals, a soft violet
accent) and **Lively Dracula** (bb's Dracula with Lively's rounder corners and springier
transitions). The motion works without either.
