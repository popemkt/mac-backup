## Shape

- `canvas-page.tsx`: 1,834 lines at the wave base, 297 lines in the worktree.
- `use-canvas-doc.ts`: owns live synchronization, history, debounce, immediate flush, and unmount flush.
- `use-canvas-keyboard.ts`: owns keyboard command routing; history, selection, clipboard, duplication, nudge, tool, and zoom commands are separate handlers.
- `use-canvas-gestures.ts`: owns tool controls, viewport transforms, stage gestures, pointer completion, and picker placement.
- `use-canvas-selection.ts`: owns selection state, inspector anchors, selected edge/shape derivation, and card/edge selection gestures.

- `canvas-edge-layer.tsx` and `canvas-card-layer.tsx`: render edges and heterogeneous canvas cards without document persistence or selection setters.
- `canvas-overlays.tsx`: renders the tool palette, selection toolbar, inspectors, and node picker through callbacks.

## Pointer reducer vocabulary

The pure reducer accepts eight events: `pan/start`, `pan/set`, `move/start`, `resize/start`, `edge/start`, `marquee/start`, `pointer/move`, and `pointer/end`. Its state represents all eight observed drag variants: pan, move-pending, move, resize-pending, resize, edge, marquee-pending, and marquee. Persistence intent remains explicit as `silent`, `history`, or `flush`.

## Preserved hidden behavior

- Random edge and native-binding IDs remain supplied at the React boundary, so the reducer is deterministic.
- Pointer capture, drag thresholding, snap guides, cursor-centered zoom, space/Alt panning, marquee additivity, off-port edge rejection, and inspector anchoring retain their existing DOM behavior.
- Live store revisions still cannot overwrite an active drag or dirty local document.
- The last document applied in a debounce window is the document persisted; dirty state still flushes on unmount.
- Native edges still validate target constraints, write a one-shot ref prop, detect missing bindings at render time, and offer to unset the prop on deletion.
- Clipboard paste and duplicate still remap node and edge IDs and offset pasted content by 24 canvas units.
