# Research: a bb-lively theme after Remedy's *Control* (2026-10-27)

The owner chose the theme: "Control the game, so supernatural, pattern, prismatic, blur and new weird
theme". This is the source research behind the **Control** preset. Its mapping to code lives in
[ART-DIRECTION.md#control](../../assets/bb-plugins/bb-lively/ART-DIRECTION.md#control), and is not
restated here. HTML-in-Canvas, which three of its effects use, is covered by the spike in
`.kb/assets/kb-html-in-canvas/2026-10-25/findings.md`.

## Source quality

- No GDC talk on Control's art direction turned up. The one GDC item found is a talk on
  procedural destruction.
- The artbook is *The Art and Making of Control* (Future Press). It was not available to read.
- Kotaku, the Control Fandom wiki and Gematsu blocked fetches, so those claims rest on search
  summaries and are marked as such.
- Claims from play are marked "from play, unsourced". Hex values are our own picks, not
  measurements.

## Findings

### 1. The Oldest House: brutalist concrete

- The brief was Brutalism, raw concrete (*béton brut*). Remedy's Maggs said Brutalism "is very
  straight, clean lines that work really well when the player is running around destroying
  things". The spaces deliberately do not make architectural sense.
  [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/exploring-the-world-driven-game-design-of-i-control-i-)
- Its models are the windowless Long Lines Building and Kahn's Yale Center for British Art, whose
  wood-and-concrete halls shaped the executive sector. Further influences:
  - Breuer;
  - Ando's light slits;
  - Scarpa's stepped forms;
  - Boston City Hall's coffered ceilings.

  [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/art/the-real-buildings-that-inspired-i-control-i-s-oldest-house)
- Materials are concrete with granite, wood, carpet and brass. Light comes from rows of large
  round lights in the lobbies, fluorescent tubes in the offices, and slits.
  [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/art/the-real-buildings-that-inspired-i-control-i-s-oldest-house)
- The technology is "cassette futurism": floppy drives, monochrome monitors, pneumatic tubes.
  [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26151/twisting-a-familiar-city-the-secrets-behind-control-resonant-design)
- A Game Informer art feature covers the overall look.
  [Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/2019/03/27/the-mesmerizing-art-behind-control)

### 2. The Federal Bureau of Control: typography and wayfinding

Per Fonts In Use, a third-party identification, not confirmed by Remedy:

- **Interstate** sets the green wayfinding signs and the HUD's objectives and locations.
- **Basic Commercial** (Linotype's Akzidenz-Grotesk) sets case files, research papers and letters.
- **ITC Avant Garde Gothic Bold** sets the large area title cards and the wordmark. The cards are
  used sparingly and stay on screen while you play.

[Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/48689/control-video-game),
[Carl Waldron](https://carlwaldron.com/the-typographic-simplicity-of-control/)

Two related claims:

- Kotaku calls the splash words "deeply sixties" and the maintenance signs highway-like (from a
  search summary). [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/the-fonts-in-control-make-its-world-feel-way-creepier-1837714580)
- All three faces are commercial. Free stand-ins are Overpass or Barlow for Interstate, Inter or
  Archivo for Akzidenz, and URW Gothic, Jost or Outfit for Avant Garde.

### 3. The Hiss

- The Hiss shows as an intense red on possessed employees, who float suspended in midair. Their
  red, fluid forms contrast with the sharp architecture.
  [Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/2019/03/27/the-mesmerizing-art-behind-control)
- Red is "a color fully claimed by the Hiss": red light, bursting red matter, and boiling screen
  effects. This is said of the sequel, carried over from the first game.
  [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/26151/twisting-a-familiar-city-the-secrets-behind-control-resonant-design),
  [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/control-resonant/interview-design-music-no-gen-ai)
- The possessed repeat an incantation without end (from a wiki summary).
  [Control wiki](https://control.fandom.com/wiki/The_Hiss?oldid=3173)
- From play, unsourced: the incantation overlaps itself on screen in layered subtitles.

### 4. The Astral Plane and the Board

- The Astral Plane is a stark white void with no horizon and sharp-edged cubic platforms. Its
  vagueness is deliberate.
  [Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/2019/03/28/remedy-breaks-down-one-of-controls-big-mysteries-the-astral-plane)
- The Board is a giant black inverted pyramid against that white.
  [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/control-the-board-pyramid/)
- The Board speaks in slash-separated alternatives, such as "bound/delivered" and
  "approved/elusive". The Former speaks a garbled, buzzing language.
  [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/control-the-board-pyramid/)

### 5. Altered Items and Objects of Power

- They look mundane and behave impossibly, which Remedy's Macdonald frames as "New Weird".
  [SVG](https://www.svg.com/673802/the-surprising-real-world-event-that-inspired-controls-objects-of-power/)
- The Service Weapon's facets waver "as though seen through water".
  [God is a Geek](https://www.godisageek.com/reviews/control-review/)

### 6. The Ashtray Maze and shifting architecture

- The Ashtray Maze is a motel-like structure that twists around the player. Rooms flip and grow,
  and each keeps one exit. It began as a "lock" on progress.
  [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/creating-control-s-ashtray-maze/),
  [Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/editorials/10-best-video-game-moments-of-2019/the-ashtray-maze-in-control/z7214)

### 7. The prismatic warp around paranatural events

- Lead VFX artist Elmeri Raitanen's takedowns are "a small, isolated distortion of the level":
  - the enemy's previous-frame position is reprojected into a trail;
  - a fluid simulation advects the trail;
  - the trail is "broken into the colors of dispersion".

  [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/art/a-closer-look-at-i-control-i-s-reality-bending-vfx)
- Effects should "get married to the scene" (Kasurinen). Reading the dispersion as an RGB split is
  our inference.

### 7a. The Hiss's dust (added for the send, 2026-10-27)

- The owner's brief: Control's send should be "that Hiss blur stuff that flows / looks like dust
  blowing in the wind, up".
- The same takedown effect (section 7) is the closest sourced reference: a trail of what was there,
  advected by a fluid and broken into dispersion colors.
  [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/art/a-closer-look-at-i-control-i-s-reality-bending-vfx);
  a community breakdown of the Hiss effects is
  [RealtimeVFX: Dissecting the "Hiss" VFX in Control](https://realtimevfx.com/t/dissecting-the-hiss-vfx-in-control/15604).
- From play, unsourced: Hissed matter dissolves into soft dark particulate that drifts up, with a
  few red sparks; edges fringe red and blue.
- What the send takes: the words become particles made from their own pixels, released by a ragged
  front, carried up and to one side by a growing swirl, softening from crisp to blur and greying to
  smoke, about one in fourteen a red ember, the edges split by dispersion. The message condenses out
  of the same dust. Dark on the white void, charcoal on concrete.

### 8. Redacted documents and the UI

- Collectible documents are set in Basic Commercial, with lines blacked out. Some redactions lift
  as you progress (a fan claim). The look recalls SCP Foundation files.
  [Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/48689/control-video-game),
  [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/870780/discussions/0/2244426186190350703)
- The UI is clean enough that its type carries the Bureau's authority.
  [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/the-fonts-in-control-make-its-world-feel-way-creepier-1837714580)
- From play, unsourced:
  - a white, minimal HUD;
  - the selected menu row inverted to white;
  - all-caps labels on thin rules.

## What the theme takes

- **Palette.** Concrete greys at night, a white void by day. Red stays the Hiss's alone. Green
  comes from the wayfinding signs.
- **Type.** Akzidenz-like for reading, an Interstate-like face for labels. No font files ship.
- **Art.** Coffered ceilings, form-tie holes, a round light, stark light shafts, two monoliths and
  the Board.
- **Dress at rest** (added 2026-10-28, after the owner found Control "any dark, blocky theme" with
  the effects off). bb's own controls take the Bureau's forms and signage, from sections 2, 4 and 8:
  - the composer as a form under corner brackets, with a red tab;
  - your messages filed as numbered directives;
  - the thread's title as a sign;
  - menus and dialogs as case files;
  - the new-thread page as the Astral Plane (the Board, monoliths, light shafts, the Bureau's name
    lettered large, as the area title cards are).

  It is paint, so it holds with every effect off.
- **Motion and effects.**
  - Precise motion: no overshoot, no tilt.
  - Dispersion is the signature effect, and it runs only on motion.
  - A redaction sweep on page transitions.
  - The Board's slash speech as the chant.
- **HTML-in-Canvas earns its place** where CSS cannot do the job:
  - the real outgoing thread breaking into slabs after it is gone;
  - red echoes of the waiting request;
  - the sent words as dust made of their real pixels (the dust needs only WebGL 2; HTML-in-Canvas
    gives it the real pixels, else it draws the text's glyphs).

  The prism over the composer on a send (`prismEdge`) gave way to the dust.

  Each has a CSS half for when the flag is off.
