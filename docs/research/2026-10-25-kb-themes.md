# kb themes: ZZZ and Transistor, and a design system that can be a whole theme

Date: 2026-10-25. Scope: kb's page (`kb ui`) on every surface: outline,
graph, canvas, and bb's side panel at 420–600px. The work is
[popemkt/kb#34](https://github.com/popemkt/kb/pull/34), not merged: the
owner tries it by hand first. kb's own homes for it:

- `DESIGN-UI.md` → Design tokens → Themes: how a theme is built.
- `ART-DIRECTION.md`: what each theme is after, and where each idea lives.

The owner asked for two whole themes, each a UX and UI overhaul rather than a
recolour, with the visuals and the motion as one art direction.

## Decision: a theme is a design system, extended

kb already had the mechanism. A design system is a contributed sheet of
layer-1 tokens, chosen by core's setting, and checked by a completeness
contract and a contrast guard. Following Rule 1, a theme extends that rather
than adding a second "theme" concept beside it. It adds:

- **Tokens a system restates**, each defaulting to kb's current look:
  - display and label faces;
  - an art image over the chrome and over a modal's scrim;
  - `corner-shape` and the lean of a tag chip;
  - the motion personality. The timing vocabulary moved from `motion.css`
    to layer 1, beside a spring easing and the keyframes for a surface's
    entry and a page's reveal.
- **`Art`**: one slot that draws the shown system's art in two planes:
  - a ground under everything;
  - a veil over everything, for passing gestures only. It is empty at rest
    and hidden under reduced motion.
- **A backdrop pairing.** The page head shows the paired backdrop while
  neither the page's node nor core's setting chooses one. A choice still
  wins.

Rejected:

- A separate theme registry, which would duplicate the design-system point.
- Graph-theme pairing for now. The themes family may not import the graph
  family, and the lens theme cannot say "unset" (kb gap
  `01M5S0HPNDEZ9RSQW4W679VP03`).

## The two themes

The topic analysis (World, Art, UI, Motion, Sound → motion, Anchors) is the
same text as in bb-lively's `ART-DIRECTION.md` (PR #75). It is copied
verbatim into kb, which depends on nothing here.

**ZZZ**:

- **Look**: a near-black street in lime and yellow; newsprint in light mode.
  Titles are Archivo in black, narrow italic capitals; labels are a wide
  italic strip.
- **Shape**: bevelled corners, 2px outlines, hard sticker shadows and
  leaning chips.
- **Motion**: the wobbly × lively `linear()` spring from the 2026-10-24
  research. Surfaces arrive as a sticker slap, and pages as a hard cut with a
  flash.
- **Art**:
  - the ground is a comic page with a marquee band;
  - the veil plays a slanted "changing channel" wipe on a page change, and
    an SFX sticker on a structural write (typing is not one);
  - the page head is a broadcast set with a TV, a REC lamp while editing,
    and static on a write.

**Transistor**:

- **Look**: a teal Cloudbank night with ivory ink; Cloudbank by day in light
  mode. Titles are Federant, labels are Josefin Sans in tracked capitals, and
  the text is Jost.
- **Shape**: scooped corners; elevation is a cyan glow.
- **Motion**: a slow settle with no overshoot. Surfaces arrive grey and warm
  into colour, the muffled-score freeze cue.
- **Art**:
  - the ground is a seeded Art Nouveau skyline with light rails, and a trail
    across it per write;
  - on a page change, the veil plays Turn(): the frame freezes under a cyan
    sheet while the bar fills;
  - the page head is a painted dusk whose windows brighten while editing.
- **Why teal, not the source's violet dusk**: a teal night keeps cyan and
  ivory at AA on every ground. The violet and magenta dusk lives in the art.

Not built yet, recorded as kb gaps and todos:

- Turn() as a plan mode that queues edits and releases them as one
  transaction (gap `01M5S0J203QE0ZMB7MGN7GJXD8`).
- Functions as palette cards (gap `01M5S0J1FVEGHTYJVY4HRS857S`).
- Limiters.
- The Process dissolve.
- ZZZ's tab swell, press squash, tile stagger and TV-wall canvas cards.
- The user art slot.

## Constraints and how they were checked

- **Copyright.** The kb repo is public. All art is original SVG and CSS
  painted with tokens. The words on it are kb's own. The faces are OFL and
  come from npm (`@fontsource*`). No game art, screenshot, logo or font is
  committed.
- **Contrast.** The contrast guard passes at AA 4.5:1 for both themes in
  light and dark, including composited tints and tag chips. ZZZ dark needed
  `--tag-ink-mix: 22%`.
- **Reduced motion.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion` no gesture is drawn,
  and ambient loops stop. Before the veil was hidden, the global 0.01ms rule
  still showed one frame of ZZZ's lime wipe, which was a flash.
  `final/zzz-dark-reduced-gesture.png` shows the fix.
- **Bundle.** The art's code loads the first time a page shows its theme.
  The eager bundle carries only the two sheets, at 1,943,705 of 1,950,000
  bytes. That leaves about 6 KB of headroom, down from about 29 KB.
- **Frame time.** Canvas pan on the owner's store copy, measured locally
  with the default, ZZZ and Transistor in turn, measured the same within
  noise: about 10fps in all three, p95 about 110ms. The machine was under
  heavy load from other work. The ground's loops are transform and opacity
  only, at periods of 12s or more.
- **Narrow panel.** At 420–600px ZZZ's broadcast head shrank its TV, which
  had covered the breadcrumb. A container query on the head fixes it.
